# Story Team Onboarding

&emsp;&emsp;Hello new story person! My name is Derek Harris, and I’m the
story lead for SIGGD this school year. I’ll keep this fairly short so I
hope you read through everything, there will be a quiz (hahaha so original).
So first let me say welcome to SIGGD, and welcome to the Story Team. If you
have any questions on the content of this article or about anything in
general, please feel free to ask me in person or over discord. Or email
I guess. After reading this you should absolutely read through the [Story
Team Standards](./story-team-standards.md) article, as well as the other articles but that one’s the
most important.

&emsp;&emsp;So obviously the most important thing with working on the story
is to know what the story is. The best resource for learning about the story
will be the [Story Design Document](./story-design-doc.md). The linked article will further explain
what that is. However, the document will probably not have everything you
need to work on specific parts of the story, so I will also have you talk to
one of the other members of Story or myself depending on what’s going on
during the first meeting you attend.

&emsp;&emsp;Once you know the story you can immediately be put to work, hooray!
There is a convenient [Task List](./story-tasks.md) that we will be using throughout the year. All
of these tasks should require clarification but it could still be nice to
familiarize yourself with the task organization.

&emsp;&emsp;I can’t wait to see you in the meetings! Once again feel free to
reach out to me with any questions. Now go read the Story Team Standards!
