# Accessing the Document

&emsp;&emsp;To access the document, go to the [Projects Page](../../projects/index.md)
and find the current project. From there, go to the story section then
the design document page.

---

# What to Expect

&emsp;&emsp;The document will explain formatting rules and style for
writing lines/scenes, as well as an overview of the full story. The
formatting can be a bit strange since it needs to work for the
programmers, but hopefully the document will be able to help clarify why
the formatting is the way it is. Commonly there will be markers for the
ends of lines and how long each line should appear on screen, but the
specifics are different for each game. The style for writing is just to
ensure a certain degree of cohesiveness between writers. These are by no
means hard and fast rules, but breaking from the style should be done
with intention. Finally, in the story overview, certain larger details
that affect most aspects of the story will also appear in their own
section for quick reference.

---

# Why It's Important

&emsp;&emsp;This document should promote consistency within the story,
and allow members to work more independently. It will also help ensure
we don’t forget details. However, this document is by no means necessary
to constantly reference, as everything in it will be decided during
meetings.

---

# How to Read Through It

&emsp;&emsp;Each section of the document will be in its own tab, with
the most important parts being highlighted in some way. I highly
recommend attending meetings over relying on the document, as this will
make it easier to write without reading through it very often. The
character motivations in the story overview will likely be the best
place to look for details generally, we’ll see when the story starts to
really get rolling though.
